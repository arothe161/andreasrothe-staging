Transformation in Traditional Structures

Reorganisation and digital transformation in public-sector organisations and established businesses often create particular challenges.

Traditional leadership roles are gradually being complemented by modern leadership methods shaped by dynamic processes, digitalisation and AI. The demands of New Work often create uncertainty for leaders. A typical question is: “Am I still allowed to tell my employees what to do, or does everything now have to come bottom-up?”

Together, we work out the importance of leadership during transition phases and in agile working environments—adapted to the individual personality and the current context.

Formats
Offsites, team workshops (1–2 days) and retrospectives
Impact
Stronger leadership and more effective, efficient teams
