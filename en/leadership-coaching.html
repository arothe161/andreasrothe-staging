Digital Transformation and New Work in Organisations and the Public Sector

Reorganisation and digital transformation in public-sector and private-sector organisations often present particular challenges.

Traditional leadership roles are increasingly complemented by modern leadership practices shaped by dynamic processes, digitalisation and AI. The demands of New Work can create uncertainty for leaders. A typical question is: “Am I still allowed to tell my team what to do, or does everything now have to come bottom-up?”

Together, we explore the role of leadership during transition and in agile working environments — tailored to your individual personality and current organisational context.

Formats
Offsites, team workshops (1–2 days) and retrospectives
Benefits
Strong leadership and more effective, higher-performing teams
